What it costs. Everything here is already in the app except one thing: the per-player STAT LINE ("16/24, 204 yd, 3 TD"). Sleeper's matchup feed carries points, not yards — the line comes from /stats/nfl/regular/{season}/{week}, one call per tick for the whole league, cached like the scores. Without it the row still works: the status line falls back to clock and score, which is what we draw today. Projected final is our own live projection (actual so far + the rest of the game's share), coloured green when he is beating the pre-game number and red when he is behind it — the same read Sleeper puts in that slot.

What B adds over A. The same row, plus the three things a scoreboard cannot tell him: the slot bar (who owns this matchup and by how much, at a glance down the column), the lock clock instead of a kickoff time, and a tell — one line, only when there is something to do or something worth knowing: a questionable starter with a bench man in the same window, or the single slot that is deciding the week. The header trades "yet to play" counts for the chip ledger, which says the same thing in the shape of a lineup, and the win probability carries where it started so the number means something.
