Why it reads calm. One bright thing per card. Today's row has four (the pill, the bolded team score, the points, the stats) and they fight; here the stat line is white, the points are white, and everything else — projection, clock, score, opponent — is the same grey at the same size, so the eye goes to the two things that change what he does.
