02 · Draft-order odds

Where you'll actually pick in 2027

The board assumes the projected finish. This runs each league's real lottery (Kreeper's reseeded drum, B&B's Chase, 7½'s two drums) on 10,000 simulated seasons and shows every team's odds at every slot — then the board can follow your most likely slot.
