What the phone does differently. A slot is a stack, not a pair: your man on top in the panel colour, the opponent's underneath a shade darker, with the position and the margin between them. Nothing scrolls sideways, nothing is truncated to fit two columns, and the stat line still fits on one line at 412px. The league strip at the top scrolls; the tab bar never moves.

Lineup leads with the decision. On the phone you are usually opening this app because something needs doing, so the lock warning is the first thing on the screen with two fat thumb targets, and the "everything else is fine" line is the small print underneath. Wire keeps the three boards behind a segmented control because they are the same list read three ways. Notifications are the two you picked — a lock warning with the swap in the body, and a win-probability flip — plus the ongoing "Game day" notice Android requires while the app checks once a minute.
