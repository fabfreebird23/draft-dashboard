B&B

1Chase for the Pick planner

Tank mode sells for next year, but in B&B the biggest pile of lottery balls doesn't go to the worst record. It goes to the winner of the consolation bracket. Selling everything now could cost you the Chase.

This puts the lottery math next to the sell list: the odds of each selection for each way the season could end, your Chase chances with and without the players you're selling, and when to sell each one.

Real: the lottery weights, the odds (simulated), your 2-10 record and the Chase field. Illustrative: the Chase-win % and the sell timing.
Lottery sim from the hub's weights (B&B's is in config.yaml) + the playoff sim for 'who's in the Chase' + a Chase-bracket sim on weeks 14–17 projections. Sell timing from Chase value vs contender demand. About a day. B&B only (Kreeper and 7½ Men have different lotteries; each could get its own).

Kreeper

3Byes & playoff-week planner

Byes and playoff weeks for every starter, through week 17, on one grid. In Kreeper it finds a real problem: week 14, the first playoff week, has Love, Pickens and McBride all on bye, and McBride is your only TE.

Each hole comes with a fix: a seller's player who plays that week, a handcuff, or a waiver stash, while there's still time to trade.

Real: every bye week and the playoff weeks. Illustrative: the cover suggestions and handcuff notes.
Bye table (already seeded) + playoff weeks (now correct, two-week rounds) + the sellers list for cover suggestions + Sleeper depth charts for handcuffs. Half a day.

4League GM board

The tank/contend switch knows your mode. This shows everyone's: playoff odds, contend/bubble/seller, their weakest starting spot, their deepest position, and the picks they still own.

Trades are matches between a seller with your need and a contender with your surplus. This makes them visible at a glance instead of one partner at a time.

Real: the playoff odds. Illustrative: needs, surplus, picks and notes.
Playoff odds (cached sim), weakest slot / deepest position per roster (optimizer), traded picks. Half a day; it's the tank/contend data turned outward.
