In-season, arranged

The app was built for a draft: one league at a time, a phase toggle, a home page counting down to something that has now happened. For the next four months the week has a shape that repeats — Tue/Wed waivers · Thu lock · Sat/Sun lineups · Sun/Mon live — and you have four teams doing it at once. Two changes carry the rest: home is one screen across every league, and the app knows what day it is.
