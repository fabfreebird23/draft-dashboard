Four looks to set Bloody Sunday apart

The same screen in each: your Kreeper week vs Tanner, the three things that need you, and the top of the rest-of-season board. Today's look is clean but reads like most dark fantasy apps; each of these commits to one idea. Names and numbers are from this week; the alert and trade lines are illustrative.

01Broadcast

Like a Sunday broadcast: a score bug, a chyron, angled team-color bars, a pulsing LIVE tag. It's at its best on game day and makes Live feel like the RedZone channel. It's loud, so the off-days need a quieter version.

Bebas Neue / Barlow Condensed · team-color washes · angled cuts · the most 'TV'.

02Front Office

A GM's war room: memos, file-folder tabs, verdict stamps (CONTENDING, SELL), a typeset big board. It fits what the app has become, a front office for keeper leagues, more than a live scoreboard. It's the calmest to read and the most different from Sleeper and ESPN.

Fraunces serif headlines + IBM Plex Mono data · warm paper on near-black · stamps for modes and verdicts.

03Terminal

Bloomberg for fantasy: players as tickers with prices (ROS points), 7-day sparklines, a scrolling tape and amber-on-black density. It suits the numbers-heavy screens (Rankings, Trades, FAAB markets) and anyone who likes a dense screen. It's a bit hard on a phone.

JetBrains Mono everywhere · amber/green/red on black · ticker tape · sparklines on every player.

04Editorial

A sports-magazine issue each week: huge condensed numerals, one cut-out player photo as the hero, cream and crimson. It's the most distinctive and the best looking for shareable screenshots and the league chat. It's less dense, so the deep tables need their own treatment.
