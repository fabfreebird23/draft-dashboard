What changed from v1: the full list instead of a sample, in tier bands with a one-line reason per tier; the who toggle (Mine + FA is the default and drops the rest of the league's rostered players so the board is only ever men you can actually start or claim); the Consensus source, which averages the three positional ranks and sorts by it — its Spread column is the disagreement, so a green 1–2 and a red 13–24 mean different things even at the same average. Colour is by third of the position on each column, the way Flock does it, so a yellow 22 at WR is not a warning, it is a WR2. Owner column keeps the league lens, with the waiver bid and what a free agent adds to your week when it is one.
