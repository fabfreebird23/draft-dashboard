Rankings · movers, small

Two ways that don't cost the board its screen. A is a one-line ticker under the control strip — the biggest moves as chips, ~36px tall, click one to jump to the row. B makes Movers a position pill: click it and the board itself becomes the movers list, sorted by the size of the move, with the day's delta under every rank and a one-line why in the owner column. Both share the "since" switch. Either way every rank cell on the normal board carries its delta.

Same grid, same columns, so nothing new to learn: the rank cells show the per-panel move instead of the rank, Consensus becomes Move, Spread becomes Now (was → is), and the owner column gets the derived why. A mover is a consensus move of 3+ (or 10% of the position) or one panel moving 6+. Ticker A and view B can coexist — A is the glance, B the list.
