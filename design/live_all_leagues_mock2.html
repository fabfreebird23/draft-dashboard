A row that is live is tinted and its clock is crimson, so an open drawer tells you at a glance which men are on the field. The bench line is the one thing the single-league tab does not show today, and it earns its place here: it is where "Mason out-scoring your FLEX by 5.4" shows up while you can still do nothing about it, but will remember next week.

How it behaves. One fragment repaints the whole screen on the cadence, so an open drawer keeps ticking and your scroll position holds. Open/closed is remembered per league for the session, and Expand all / Collapse all set every one at once. On a phone the row stacks and the drawer's two lineups become one after the other, yours first. Cost is the same four league reads Home already does — opening a drawer adds nothing, because the lineups are in the data that was already fetched.
