01Today becomes an inbox

Today shows four league rows, each with its own Decide or Fix lineup button, and you open each one to find out what it wants. This turns them into one list of the things that need you, across all four leagues, in the order they lock, each with the one action that resolves it. A league with nothing to do gets a single quiet line instead of a button.

The four win probabilities shrink to a strip at the bottom. They're worth a glance, but they aren't jobs.

Built from what Command Center already works out per league (lock warnings, the top claim, the swap). New work: merging four leagues into one list and sorting it by lock time. Server-side, roughly a day.

02Keepers shows the whole ladder

The keeper bugs we just fixed were invisible because the screen showed only one number per player. This shows each player's price for every year he can be kept: Walker R3 now, R1 in 2028, then ADP. Year 3's "ADP, by rule" is printed on the card, so you can check the price instead of trusting it.

A tray at the top holds the five players the board would keep (3 regular + 2 rookie, faces and rounds), so the answer comes before the reasoning.

The prices already come out of keeper_outlook. New work: running it one and two years ahead, plus the tray. About a day.

03Waivers becomes a claim builder

Wire shows one claim card at a time, and the order, bids and drops are left for you to work out in Sleeper. This builds the whole Wednesday: claims in priority order (drag to reorder), a bid per claim chosen from the Ballers' range, who to drop for each, and a budget bar showing $94 now and $68 if everything clears.

A contingency claim (if #1 fails) is a first-class row, because that's how FAAB actually gets won. Sleeper still takes the claims; this hands it a finished plan.

Uses the FAAB fix from this week, the Ballers' bid ranges and the free-agent gain pass. New work: a saved claim queue per league, and reordering on the phone. One to two days. It needs care: a wrong drop costs a player.

04Live shows the net, not the noise

You have Gibbs in one league and face him in another. Right now a Gibbs touchdown is good news on one screen and bad news on the next, and the alerts fire for both. This feed nets each play across all four leagues: Gibbs's TD is +6.1 in B&B and −6.1 in Kreeper, so net 0.0, and it says so once.

The header becomes one number for your whole Sunday (net +14.6), which no single league screen can give you.

The exposure join (who is yours and who is against you, per league) already exists for Live · all. New work: a per-play feed and netting. The phone alerts can use the same net, so a play that cancels out never buzzes. About a day.
