Why every variant is on tabular figures +49   +45   +112   +52 proportional — the column wobbles
  +49   +45   +112   +52 tabular — digits share a width, so the column is a column

My pick is 3 — Sora names, Inter numbers on tnum. Sora is already the app's display face, so the row is tied to the rest of the design and there is no third webfont to load; the name reads as a name and the numbers read as a table. If the panel has felt too SMALL rather than too tall, take 6 instead — same treatment, two points up, three fewer rows.
