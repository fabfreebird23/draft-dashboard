Rankings · movers

A section at the top of Rankings that answers who moved. Each panel is snapshotted once a day (and again on Sunday morning after inactives), so a rise or fall is against yesterday, or against the start of the week. The point is to catch the guy the panels are quietly turning on — a rise on all three panels that the wire hasn't priced yet — and the starter they're bailing on.

What makes a mover worth showing: the consensus moved 3+ places (or 10% of the position), or one panel moved 6+. The why line is derived, not typed: an injury status change on the player (Kupp OUT), a status change on a teammate at the same position (Skattebo → Tracy), or "one panel alone" when only one moved. Sparkline is the four snapshots of the week. Risers who are free agents and would start for you get the wire number; fallers who are your starters name the cover.

Same board as before, with the day's delta under every rank cell and the consensus move on the player pill. A "since" switch (yesterday · Tuesday · last week) drives both this and the movers section, and the movers section collapses to a one-line band ("nobody moved more than 2") on a quiet day. Snapshots are cheap — the panels are already cached to disk hourly; this keeps one copy per day per panel, ~50 KB each.
