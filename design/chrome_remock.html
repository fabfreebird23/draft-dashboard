Sorting by a column is the natural way to switch source on a board — it is what every spreadsheet does — and it frees the control strip to just who + position. The lit header carries a ▾ and the sorted column gets a faint outline so the sort is readable from across the room. Spread and Opp are not sortable (grey, no dot). The Lineup tab's "Rank by" strip stays a strip, because there it changes the recommendation rather than the sort.
